DINO DASH

You are a lone runner in a purple ruin. Slow dinosaurs plod after you, cats hunt fast, and eagles fly straight over the walls. Mine three relics to open the exit.

WHO CHASES YOU

  • Dinosaurs — slow, but they never lose your scent.
  • Cats — quick and twitchy, they cut corners.
  • Eagles — they ignore walls and fly straight at you.
  • Freeze or Smash one and it breaks apart. Run into a frozen enemy and it is gone for the rest of the level.

WHAT YOU COLLECT

  • Relics — stand on one to mine it. Three clears the level and each one pays a power.
  • Coins — every tenth coin makes the runner jump and shout "Yay!".
  • A relic wears the colour of the power it pays. One red Smash relic hides in every level.

THE FIVE POWERS

  • Smash — 8s. Touch an enemy and it is destroyed.
  • Freeze — 6s. Everything stops, and stopped enemies break.
  • Scare — 8s. They flee, and a touch sends one home.
  • Speed — 8s. You outrun everything but the eagles.
  • Shield — 10s. A hit costs you nothing.

CONTROLS

  • ← ↑ ↓ → or WASD to run.
  • P pauses. M mutes the music.
  • On a phone, swipe the maze or use the pad above.
Every maze is braided: no tunnel ends in a wall, so there is always a way out of a chase. Seven ruins, each larger and meaner than the last. Powers last a few seconds — spend them moving, not hiding.
